# c-manager v1.1.0

This release adds direct category filtering to the source columns.

## Concepts

The Concepts column now has a compact category selector in its header.

Choose a category to display only concepts assigned to that category, or switch back to **All categories**.

## Music

The Music column has the same independent category selector.

Choose a music category to display only matching tracks.

## Additional behavior

- **Uncategorized** is available in both filters.
- The filters do not modify or delete data.
- Concept and Music filters are independent.
- Random selection uses only the concepts and music currently visible through these filters.
