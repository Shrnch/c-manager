# c-manager v1.4.7

Calendar now includes a **Show progress** checkbox.

When enabled, the Calendar works exactly as before.

When disabled, it becomes a cleaner planning view and shows only:

- **Execution planned**
- **Publication planned — still in progress**
- **Ready to publish**

Completed, Published and resolved/historical stages are hidden from the Calendar and Day Overview until Show progress is enabled again.

The setting is remembered locally.
